## 6. Anonymity & Internal Records

Posts and comments on Uocial are displayed publicly without any identifying
information attached. Your real identity is not visible to other users or to
moderators.

**Pseudonyms within a post thread:** When you comment on a post, a consistent
pseudonym is displayed next to each of your comments within that thread. This
pseudonym is a cryptographic hash derived from your account and the post
identifier; it is different for every post and cannot be reversed to identify
you. Its purpose is to let readers follow a conversation by seeing which
replies belong to the same commenter, without revealing who that commenter is.
Additionally, if you comment on your own post, your comments will be labelled
to indicate that you are the author of that post, so other users can
distinguish the original poster's replies from those of other commenters.

However, the Platform's internal database, accessible only to the
administrator, does contain a link between your account and the content you
have created. This is necessary to maintain platform integrity and to comply
with legal obligations if required.
This internal record is treated as strictly confidential and will not be
disclosed except as required by law (see Section 10).

## 7. Content Moderation

The Platform uses a combination of automated and human moderation to protect
its community.

**Automated mechanism:** When a post or comment receives a sufficient number of
user reports, it is automatically hidden pending review by a human moderator.
This is a temporary precautionary measure only. The moderator then makes the
final decision on whether the content should remain hidden or be restored. No
final decision is made solely by automated means (Article 22 GDPR does not
apply).

**Proactive moderator action:** A moderator may also hide a post or comment
directly, without a report threshold being reached, if its content is
determined to violate the Terms of Use or to be harmful to the community. This
decision is made by a human moderator based on the content alone; moderators
do not have access to user identity.

Both mechanisms operate under the Platform's legitimate interest in maintaining
a safe environment (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 8. Cookies and Local Storage

The Platform uses the following cookies:

| Cookie type        | Purpose                                                    | Duration                                                       |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Session cookie     | Keeps you logged in during your visit                      | Deleted when your session ends (maximum 2 hours of inactivity) |
| Remember me cookie | Keeps you logged in across browser sessions                | 400 days                                                       |
| XSRF-TOKEN         | Protects against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks | Matches session lifetime                                       |

The Platform also uses your browser's **localStorage** to store the following:

| Item                  | Purpose                              | Duration                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Interface preferences | Stores your local interface settings | Persistent until you clear your browser's local storage |

The session cookie, XSRF-TOKEN, and localStorage entry are strictly necessary
for the Platform to function and do not require consent under applicable law.
The remember me cookie is optional and is only set if you explicitly opt in by
checking "Remember me" at login. You may clear cookies at any time through your
browser settings (clearing the session cookie will log you out), and you may
clear localStorage through your browser's developer tools. None of these cookies
track you across other websites or are used for advertising or analytics.

## 9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

If you request deletion of your account, your publicly visible profile and
display name will be removed from the Platform promptly. Your IP addresses are
also deleted at that time: IP addresses stored in active session records are
deleted within 2 hours of session expiry, and IP addresses in server access
logs are deleted within 52 days in the ordinary course (or earlier upon a
deletion request).

Your email address, user account record, and the content you have created
(posts, comments, and reactions to posts and comments) are retained for a
period of five (5) years from the date of deletion. This retention period
corresponds to the five-year statute of limitations for civil damages claims
under Greek law (Article 937 of the Greek Civil Code) and the prescription
period for criminal offences under Article 111 of the Greek Penal Code, and is
permitted under Article 17(3)(b) of the GDPR as data necessary for the
establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

After this five-year period, your email address, display name, and any
remaining direct identifiers are permanently deleted from your account record.
Your posts, comments, and reactions — which at that point contain no
information capable of identifying you — are retained as permanently anonymous
content. Once anonymised, this data no longer constitutes personal data under
the GDPR, and you will not be identifiable as the author of or reactor to any
content.

If you stop using the Platform without requesting deletion, your data will be
retained for as long as your account exists, and for five (5) years thereafter,
after which the same anonymisation process applies (posts, comments, and
reactions are retained as anonymous data; all identifiers are deleted).
To request account deletion or inquire about your data, please contact us at
privacy@uocial.gr.

## 12. Your Rights Under the GDPR

As a data subject under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

- Right of access (Article 15): you may request a copy of the personal data we
  hold about you;
- Right to rectification (Article 16): you may request correction of inaccurate
  data;
- Right to erasure (Article 17): you may request deletion of your account and
  publicly visible data. Your IP addresses are deleted promptly. Your email
  address, account record, and content are retained for five (5) years as
  permitted under Article 17(3)(b), as this data may be necessary for the
  establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims. After this period, your
  email address and identifiers are permanently deleted; your posts, comments,
  and reactions are retained as permanently anonymous content that cannot be
  attributed to you;
- Right to restriction of processing (Article 18): you may request that we
  limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances;
- Right to data portability (Article 20): you may request a copy of the
  personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used,
  machine-readable format;
- Right to object (Article 21): you may object to processing based on
  legitimate interests;
- Right to lodge a complaint: you have the right to lodge a complaint with the
  Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA) at www.dpa.gr.

To exercise any of your rights, please contact us at privacy@uocial.gr. We will
respond within one month of receipt. In cases of complexity or a high volume of
requests, this period may be extended by a further two months, in which case we
will notify you.
