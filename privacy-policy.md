## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Uocial ("we", "our", "the Platform")
collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal data of its users. Uocial is
operated by a private individual and is independent of any university.
Uocial is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with Regulation
(EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR") and applicable
Greek national law. Please read this policy carefully.

## 2. Data Controller

The data controller for your personal information is the administrator of
Uocial, a private individual based in Greece. For all privacy-related
inquiries, please contact:

Email: privacy@uocial.gr

## 3. What Data We Collect

### 3.1 Data You Provide

- University email address (used for registration and verification);
- A display name of your choice (this does not need to be your real name and is
  used solely to identify you within the Platform);
- Any content you post, including posts and comments.

### 3.2 Data Automatically Collected

- Technical data such as IP address and browser/device information, collected
  for security and platform integrity purposes;
- Timestamps of content creation and account activity.

### 3.3 Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

- Government-issued identification documents;
- Payment information;
- Location data;
- Data for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

- To verify your eligibility as a university student;
- To operate, maintain, and secure the Platform;
- To link content to accounts for the purposes of moderation and legal
  compliance;
- To comply with legal obligations under applicable law.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.
We do not sell, share, or rent your data to any third party.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

- Contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b)): processing your email address and
  account data is necessary to provide the service you registered for;
- Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): retaining technical data (e.g., IP
  logs) to protect the security and integrity of the Platform;
- Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): disclosing information to law enforcement
  when required by a valid legal instrument.

## 6. Anonymity & Internal Records

Posts and comments on Uocial are displayed publicly without any identifying
information attached. Your identity is not visible to other users or to
moderators.
However, the Platform's internal database, accessible only to the
administrator, does contain a link between your account and the content you
have created. This is necessary to maintain platform integrity and to comply
with legal obligations if required.
This internal record is treated as strictly confidential and will not be
disclosed except as required by law (see Section 8).

## 7. Data Retention

Your personal data is retained for as long as your account exists on the
Platform and for such additional period as may be required to comply with legal
obligations or resolve disputes.
Currently, the Platform does not offer account deletion. If you stop using the
Platform, your data will be retained in accordance with this policy. If you
wish to inquire about your data, please contact us at privacy@uocial.gr.

## 8. Disclosure to Law Enforcement

Uocial does not share user data with any third parties, except where required
by a valid legal obligation. In such cases — for example, a court order or a
formal request from a Greek or EU law enforcement authority — we may disclose
the minimum amount of personal data legally required.
Examples of situations that may trigger such disclosure include, but are not
limited to: credible threats of suicide or self-harm, threats to third parties,
or other serious criminal conduct.
We will not voluntarily provide data beyond what is strictly required by the
applicable legal instrument.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Uocial does not currently use any third-party services that process user data
(such as analytics providers, advertising networks, or cloud-based email
providers). All data is handled directly by the Platform's infrastructure,
operated and funded privately by the administrator.
Should this change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated
accordingly and users will be informed.

## 10. Your Rights Under the GDPR

As a data subject under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

- Right of access (Article 15): you may request a copy of the personal data we
  hold about you;
- Right to rectification (Article 16): you may request correction of inaccurate
  data;
- Right to restriction of processing (Article 18): you may request that we
  limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances;
- Right to object (Article 21): you may object to processing based on
  legitimate interests;
- Right to lodge a complaint: you have the right to lodge a complaint with the
  Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA) at www.dpa.gr.

Please note that due to the nature of the Platform (no account deletion,
internal identity linkage for legal purposes), certain rights — such as the
right to erasure — may be subject to limitations under applicable law.
To exercise any of your rights, please contact us at privacy@uocial.gr.

## 11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your
personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Access to the
internal database containing identity-linked data is restricted exclusively to
the Platform administrator.
However, no system can guarantee absolute security. You use the Platform at
your own risk, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your
account.

## 12. Children & Minimum Age

Uocial is not intended for individuals under the age of 17. By registering,
you confirm that you are at least 17 years of age. If we become aware that a
user is under the minimum age, we will take steps to restrict their access.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes
will be communicated to users through the Platform. Continued use of the
Platform following any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
